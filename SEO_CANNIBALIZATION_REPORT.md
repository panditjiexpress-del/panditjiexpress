## 1. Executive Summary

Keyword cannibalization occurs when multiple URLs within a domain compete for identical search intents, splitting topical equity and confusing search engine ranking algorithms. 

In Pandit Ji Express, every page has been assigned a distinct search intent, audience profile, and primary canonical keyword. A strict separation exists between:
1. **Commercial Transactional Pages** (focused on booking a priest).
2. **Informational Guide Pages** (focused on educational vidhi, mantras, and checklists).
3. **Locality Authority Hubs** (focused on geographic logistics and neighborhood coverage).
4. **Cultural Specializations** (focused on regional diaspora languages and traditions).
