## 1. Service Page Role & Standard Architecture

Every service page in the repository functions as a comprehensive transactional and educational pillar, containing:
1. **Clear SEO Title & Meta Description** (including Bengaluru geographic anchor).
2. **Single Focused H1** identifying the specific ritual.
3. **AEO Direct Answer Box** defining the purpose, duration, and vidhi.
4. **Who the Service is For:** Diaspora and local families seeking authentic Vedic Karmakanda.
5. **Ritual Scope & Step-by-Step Vidhi:** Classical Shastric steps (Gauri-Ganesh, Kalash Sthapana, Navagraha, Mukhya Puja, Havan, Aarti, Ashirwad).
6. **Preparation & Materials (Two-Tier Model):**
   - Tier 1: Fresh perishables prepared by family (fruits, flowers, milk, paan leaves).
   - Tier 2: Specialized ceremonial samagri hand-carried by the priest (samidha, pure cow ghee, havan kund, gangajal, dhoop).
7. **Apartment Safety & Environmental Protocols:** Low-smoke mango samidha, elevated havan kund, balcony/living room ventilation management.
8. **Transparent Dakshina Guidelines:** Realistic customary ranges without artificial quotes or fake discounts.
9. **Geographic Coverage:** Highlighting service availability across all Bangalore sectors.
10. **Interactive FAQ Accordion:** Minimum 5–6 ritual and scheduling questions.
11. **JSON-LD Schema:** `@graph` containing `Service` + `BreadcrumbList` + `FAQPage` + `Provider` reference.

## 3. Strict Prohibitions & Safeguards

In accordance with our strict factual truth policy:
* **No Price Fabrication:** Dakshina is quoted as customary ranges (e.g. ₹3,100–₹5,100 for standard home rituals), never arbitrary discount claims.
* **No Artificial Reviews:** Only verifiable client feedback and real testimonials.
* **No Smoke Alarm / Detector Bypass Claims:** We strictly teach cross-ventilation, dry samidha, and society safety compliance; we never suggest covering or bypassing apartment fire systems.
