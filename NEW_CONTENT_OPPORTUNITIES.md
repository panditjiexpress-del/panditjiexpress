## 1. Executive Screening Methodology

Every potential topic was screened through our 4-Stage Intent Verification Filter:
1. **Existing Owner Check:** Does an existing page on `panditjiexpress.in` already rank or target this intent? (If YES &rarr; IMPROVE existing page, DO NOT create new page).
2. **Search Intent Boundary:** Is this a genuinely separate user problem (e.g., ancestral Tarpan vs regular Havan)?
3. **Information Gain:** Does the proposed topic introduce unique Vedic, logistical, or local knowledge that cannot fit naturally into an existing guide?
4. **Business Verifiability:** Can Pandit Ji Express fulfill and substantiate this service in Bangalore without fabricating credentials or capabilities?
