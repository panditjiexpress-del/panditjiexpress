# Future Locality Architecture & Scaffolds: Pandit Ji Express

This document outlines the publication roadmap for future Bangalore locality landing pages to adhere strictly to Google's Search Quality Guidelines against "doorway pages" and thin content duplication.

---

## The Locality Page Quality Rule

> **Quality Standard**: A locality landing page must ONLY be published if it contains:
> 1. Genuinely distinct local geographic context (specific gated societies, major roads, apartment complexes).
> 2. Specific residential considerations (e.g., high-rise balcony havan protocols vs. individual villa courtyard setups).
> 3. Real neighborhood travel time and dispatch logistics from the base in Jakkur / Srirampura.
> 4. Unique local customer questions (FAQ) and genuine community references.
>
> **Under no circumstances should pages be mass-generated by simply replacing the city/area keyword.**

## Pre-Publication Checklist for New Locality Pages

Before publishing any new locality HTML file to the website root:
- [ ] Ensure at least 300 words of unique, non-templated text specific to the neighborhood.
- [ ] Add the page URL to `sitemap.xml`.
- [ ] Update the `BreadcrumbList` and `LocalBusiness` JSON-LD schema with the specific neighborhood name in `areaServed`.
- [ ] Verify exactly one `<h1>` tag with targeted keyword and unique meta description.
- [ ] Test on mobile (375px) and ensure sticky CTA bar and WhatsApp pre-fill function properly.
