# Pandit Ji Express — Search Intent Governance & Query Ownership Map (Phase 1)

**Domain:** `https://panditjiexpress.in`  
**Market:** Bangalore Metropolitan Region (Karnataka)  
**Governing Standard:** 1 Primary Query &rarr; 1 Search Intent &rarr; 1 Canonical Owner URL. Zero Intent Cannibalization.

---

## 1. Intent Categories & Content Architecture Rules

1. **Transactional (BOFU — Bookings & Priest Reservations):**
   * *User Mindset:* "I have a scheduled ceremony date and need to reserve a verified Vedic pandit now."
   * *Canonical Owner:* Direct Service Pillar Pages (`/griha-pravesh-pooja-bangalore`, `/wedding-pandit-bangalore`, etc.).
2. **Local Transactional (Hyper-Local BOFU):**
   * *User Mindset:* "I live in Whitefield, HSR, E-City, or Sarjapur and need a priest who arrives punctually."
   * *Canonical Owner:* Dedicated Locality Hubs (`/north-indian-pandit-{locality}`).
3. **Commercial Investigation (MOFU — Evaluation & Cultural Fit):**
   * *User Mindset:* "Can this pandit speak my dialect (Hindi/Bihari/Maithil)? How much does it cost? How do I book?"
   * *Canonical Owner:* Cultural Hubs & Commercial Guides (`/hindi-speaking-pandit-bangalore`, `/pandit-cost-bangalore`, `/how-to-book-pandit-bangalore`).
4. **Informational (TOFU — Educational Vidhi & Checklists):**
   * *User Mindset:* "What are the steps of Griha Pravesh? What are the 7 wedding vows? What samagri do I need?"
   * *Canonical Owner:* In-Depth Guides & Checklists (`/griha-pravesh-puja-bangalore-guide`, `/puja-samagri-list-bangalore`).
5. **Navigational & Brand Assurance (Trust & E-E-A-T):**
   * *User Mindset:* "Who is Pandit Shyam Sundar? Where is Pandit Ji Express located?"
   * *Canonical Owner:* Profile, About, Contact, Gallery Pages (`/pandit-shyam-sundar`, `/about`, `/contact`).
