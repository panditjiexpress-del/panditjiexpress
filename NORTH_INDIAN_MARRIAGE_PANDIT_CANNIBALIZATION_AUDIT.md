## 1. Executive Summary & Intent Classification

A thorough audit was executed across all static HTML pages, JSON-LD schemas, GSC performance metrics, and the Phase 1 SEO governance framework (`SEO_MASTER_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `SEO_SEARCH_INTENT_MAP.md`, `BLOG_CLUSTER_MAP.md`, `SEO_CANNIBALIZATION_REPORT.md`).

### The Non-Negotiable Principle:
> **ONE PRIMARY SEARCH INTENT = ONE PRIMARY CANONICAL OWNER**  
> We do not create competing pages targeting essentially identical commercial or informational search intent. The objective is topical authority concentration, not indiscriminate URL proliferation.

  * *User Goal:* Finalizing wedding date/muhurat, booking/hiring a Gurukul-trained North Indian priest in Bengaluru, discussing dakshina/samagri/venue arrangements.
  * *Legitimate Canonical Owner:* **`/wedding-pandit-bangalore`** (Service Pillar).

  * *User Goal:* Understanding ritual chronology, spiritual symbolism, family duties, and questions to ask.
