Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Pandit Ji Express, we hold the trust of our devotees and families with the utmost sacred reverence. When you contact us to schedule a Vedic ritual, Griha Pravesh, Hawan, or Wedding ceremony, or order doorstep pooja samagri, we treat all your personal, residential, and spiritual information with strict confidentiality.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact Pandit Ji Express via phone, WhatsApp, or our website booking forms, we collect only the essential details required to coordinate your ceremony:

  • Contact Details: Your full name, mobile number, and optional email address.
  • Ceremony Preferences: The ceremony requested, preferred date, and auspicious muhurat timing.
  • Location: Your residential, apartment, or venue address in Bangalore for priest arrival and samagri delivery.
  • Ritual Preferences (Optional): Family gotra, community traditions (e.g. UP, Bihar, Marwari, Rajasthani), or custom deity requests.

2. How Your Information is Used

Your details are used strictly for service fulfillment:

  • Direct scheduling and coordination with Pandit Shyam Sundar Ji for your ceremony.
  • Timely delivery of pre-sorted, shuddh pooja samagri kits to your Bangalore doorstep.
  • Sending reminders and auspicious muhurat details before your ceremony via WhatsApp or phone.

We DO NOT sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal details to third-party telemarketers or external advertising companies.

3. Direct Devotee Communication

All communications are conducted directly via phone (+91 90657 88789) or official WhatsApp messaging. We do not use spam robocalls or automated unsolicited messaging campaigns.

4. Contact & Grievance Redressal

If you have any questions regarding your data or wish to have your contact details updated or removed from our records, please reach out to us directly:
