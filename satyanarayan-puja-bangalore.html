Satyanarayan Puja Pandit in Bangalore

Book experienced North Indian pandits for Shri Satyanarayan Swami Puja & Katha in Bangalore. Clear narration in Hindi, Vedic pujan vidhi, Panchamrit preparation, and doorstep samagri.

Shri Satyanarayan Puja is an ancient, beloved ritual derived from the Reva Kanda of the Skanda Purana. Dedicated to Lord Vishnu in his embodiment as Truth (Satya Narayana), this puja brings mental tranquility, prosperity, family harmony, and divine blessings. It is performed across North Indian households on Purnima, after achieving important life milestones, or as thanksgiving.

Ceremony Flow & Ritual Sequence

  • Ganesh & Gauri Sthapana: Removal of obstacles and initial purification with holy water and Vedic mantras.
  • Navagraha & Lokpal Pujan: Invocations to the nine planetary bodies and cardinal guardians for cosmic balance.
  • Shri Satyanarayan Kalash Sthapana: Setting up the sacred altar with betel leaves, unbroken rice, yellow cloth, and Shaligram / Vishnu Murti.
  • Narration of 5 Katha Chapters in Hindi: Pandit Ji narrates the five moral and spiritual stories with devotional explanation so elders and children alike can absorb the wisdom.
  • Havan (Optional) & Maha Aarti: Offering oblations followed by the grand singing of "Jai Lakshmi Ramana" aarti and distribution of Charan-Amrit and Panjiri prasad.

Samagri Required

Pandit Ji Express can bring the complete authentic samagri kit to your doorstep. You will only need to prepare fresh Panchamrit (milk, curd, honey, ghee, sugar) and the roasted wheat-flour Panjiri prasad at home.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best day to perform Satyanarayan Puja?

Purnima (full moon) is the most traditional day. However, it can be performed on Ekadashi, Sankranti, Thursdays, or any auspicious family occasion.

How long does the puja take?

Typically 1.5 to 2 hours. If combined with havan, it takes approximately 2 to 2.5 hours.

Can you perform it in an apartment living room?

Yes, Satyanarayan Puja requires very simple clean floor space (a chowki/low table) and is completely apartment-friendly.
