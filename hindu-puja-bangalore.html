Hindu Puja in Bangalore: Most Common Home Ceremonies Performed by North Indian Families

By Pandit Shyam Sundar •Updated: September 2026 •Reading Time: 10 mins
Direct Answer: The most commonly performed Hindu pujas in Bangalore homes include Satyanarayan Katha, Griha Pravesh Puja, Ganesh Puja, Navagraha Havan, Vastu Shanti Puja, Navratri Durga Puja, Rudrabhishek, and Shodasha Sanskara ceremonies such as Naamkaran, Mundan, and Annaprashan. North Indian families in Bengaluru arrange these through experienced Hindi-speaking Vedic priests.

Bangalore's rapidly growing North Indian community — professionals from UP, Bihar, Jharkhand, Rajasthan, Punjab, and Uttarakhand — continues to celebrate Vedic heritage with regular home ceremonies. Whether you have moved into a new apartment in Whitefield, welcomed a family member, or wish to express gratitude, finding the right North Indian pandit in Bangalore is the essential first step.

1. Satyanarayan Katha — The Most Popular Home Puja in Bangalore

Satyanarayan Katha is performed by North Indian families for virtually every auspicious occasion: moving into a new home, completing a major purchase, a child's first birthday, or as simple thanksgiving. The ceremony includes Kalash Sthapana, Ganesh Puja, recitation of five chapters of Satyanarayan Katha Granth, and Prasad distribution (panchamrit and panjiri). Duration: 2–3 hours. Can be performed in any room of an apartment.

2. Griha Pravesh Puja — Sacred Home Entry Ceremony

Griha Pravesh Puja marks auspicious first entry into a new home. The ritual includes Vastu Puja, Gauri-Ganesh Sthapana, Navagraha invocation, Vastu Havan, Kalash Yatra, and auspicious Muhurat entry. Duration: 4–6 hours. Read the dedicated Griha Pravesh guide for Bangalore apartments for apartment-specific guidance. Families across Electronic City, Sarjapur Road, and HSR Layout regularly book this ceremony.

3. Ganesh Puja — Auspicious Beginning for New Ventures

Ganesh Puja precedes every new undertaking — business launch, academic year, property registration, or new job. Duration: 1–2 hours. The ceremony includes Shodashopachara Pujan, Modak Naivedya, and Aarti. A Ganesh Havan can be added for deeper sanctification.

4. Navagraha Shanti Havan — Planetary Harmony Ceremony

Navagraha Shanti Havan is performed when a family member faces repeated obstacles, career challenges, or when an astrologer recommends planetary remedies. The nine-planet Havan includes specific mantras and Ahuti materials for each Graha. Duration: 3–4 hours. For apartment safety, see our apartment havan guide.

5. Vastu Shanti Puja — Harmonizing Your Living Space

Vastu Shanti Puja neutralizes Vastu Doshas and balances Pancha Tattva within the home. Popular for families moving into resale flats with irregular layouts. The ceremony includes Vastu Yantra Sthapana, Navagraha Pujan, and a Havan. Duration: 3–5 hours.

6. Rudrabhishek — Consecration of Lord Shiva

Rudrabhishek is performed on Shiva Lingam with Panchamrit Abhishek (milk, curd, honey, ghee, sugarwater), Bilva Patra offering, and recitation of Sri Rudram. Popular on Mondays, Mahashivratri, and Shravan Somvar. Duration: 1–2 hours.

Shodasha Sanskara Ceremonies

The sixteen Vedic life-stage ceremonies regularly performed by North Indian families across Bangalore:

  • Naamkaran (Naming Ceremony): Performed on the 11th day after birth, choosing the baby's name per birth Nakshatra and Rashi.
  • Annaprashan (First Rice Ceremony): Baby's first solid food offered as Naivedya in the 6th month.
  • Mundan (First Haircut): Performed between ages 1–3, symbolizing removal of past-life karma.
  • Upanayanam / Janeu Ceremony: Sacred thread ceremony initiating a boy into Vedic learning.
