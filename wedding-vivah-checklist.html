Frequently Asked Questions on Wedding Vivah Samagri

What is Lawa / Kheel and why is it essential for North Indian weddings?
Lawa (puffed paddy or Aralu) represents prosperity, fertile abundance, and mutual responsibility. During the Laja Homa ritual, the bride's brother pours puffed paddy into her cupped hands, which she and the groom offer jointly into the sacred Agni as an invocation for familial harmony.
How are North Indian wedding rituals conducted in Bangalore resort venues?
