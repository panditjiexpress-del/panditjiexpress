## 1. Search Intent Classifications & SERP Architecture

Search queries targeting Hindu priest services in Bangalore fall into 5 distinct psychological search intents:

1. **Transactional (High-Intent Conversion / BOFU):**
   * *User Mindset:* "I have an upcoming Griha Pravesh or Wedding on a specific muhurat date. I need to reserve a verified North Indian pandit right now."
   * *Requirements:* Prominent pricing transparency, clear ceremony inclusions, verified priest credentials, direct click-to-call (`+91 90657 88789`), WhatsApp floating CTA, and instant booking modal.
   * *Target URLs:* `/`, `/wedding-pandit-bangalore`, `/griha-pravesh-pooja-bangalore`, `/satyanarayan-puja-bangalore`, `/havan-yagna-bangalore`, `/ganesh-puja-bangalore`, `/durga-puja-navratri-bangalore`, `/rudrabhishek-bangalore`, `/naamkaran-ceremony-bangalore`, `/mundan-ceremony-bangalore`, `/annaprashan-bangalore`, `/upanayanam-janeu-bangalore`, `/booking`.

2. **Local Transactional (Micro-Geographic / BOFU):**
   * *User Mindset:* "I live in Whitefield, HSR Layout, or Marathahalli and need a pandit ji who can arrive early in the morning without being delayed by Bangalore traffic."
   * *Requirements:* Hyper-local geographical grounding, apartment clusters mentioned (e.g. Prestige Shantiniketan, Brigade Metropolis), transit-aware scheduling, local customer reassurance.
   * *Target URLs:* `/north-indian-pandit-whitefield`, `/north-indian-pandit-hsr-layout`, `/north-indian-pandit-marathahalli`.

3. **Commercial Investigation (Evaluation & Cultural Nuance / MOFU):**
   * *User Mindset:* "Can this priest speak Hindi, Bhojpuri, or Maithili? Do they know our specific regional rituals? What pujas do they perform?"
   * *Requirements:* Clear breakdown of linguistic fluency, regional ritual distinctions (Bihari Chhath, Mithila Dviraagaman, Awadhi vivah rites), service directory overviews, samagri kit options.
   * *Target URLs:* `/services`, `/hindi-speaking-pandit-bangalore`, `/bihari-pandit-bangalore`, `/maithil-pandit-bangalore`, `/online-pandit-bangalore`, `/samagri`, `/pandit-cost-bangalore`.

4. **Informational (Cornerstone Guides & Checklists / TOFU):**
   * *User Mindset:* "What is the procedure for Griha Pravesh in Bangalore? What are the 7 vows of Hindu marriage? What samagri do I need to buy? How much should I budget?"
   * *Requirements:* Authoritative, long-form Shastric depth (1,500–4,000 words), step-by-step breakdown, milk-boiling guidance, apartment ventilation safety, Vedic mantras explained, downloadable/printable checklists, contextual internal links to commercial booking pages.
   * *Target URLs:* `/best-north-indian-pandit-bangalore`, `/griha-pravesh-puja-bangalore-guide`, `/north-indian-wedding-rituals-bangalore`, `/puja-samagri-list-bangalore`, `/how-to-book-pandit-bangalore`, `/gallery`.

5. **Navigational & Brand Assurance (Trust & Governance):**
   * *User Mindset:* "Who is the lead priest behind Pandit Ji Express? How do I contact them? What is their policy?"
   * *Requirements:* Real human biography (Pandit Shyam Sundar), verified temple lineage (Varanasi roots), phone numbers, physical Bangalore coordination address, transparent terms.
   * *Target URLs:* `/pandit-shyam-sundar`, `/about`, `/contact`, `/blog`, `/privacy`, `/areas-we-serve`.

## 3. Query Intent Conflict Resolution Rules

1. **Rule 1: Commercial Keywords Belong Strictly to Pillars:**
   If a user query includes words like `"hire"`, `"book"`, `"charges"`, or `"pandit in [area]"`, the query must resolve to a Commercial Pillar (`/wedding-pandit-bangalore`, `/griha-pravesh-pooja-bangalore`) or Locality Page (`/north-indian-pandit-whitefield`). Guides must NOT target commercial head terms in their primary title or H1.
2. **Rule 2: Educational Queries Belong Strictly to Blog Guides:**
   If a query includes `"meaning"`, `"steps"`, `"why"`, `"process"`, `"rules"`, or `"list"`, the query must resolve to an Informational Guide (`/north-indian-wedding-rituals-bangalore`, `/griha-pravesh-puja-bangalore-guide`, `/puja-samagri-list-bangalore`). Service pillars must NOT attempt to become 4,000-word encyclopedic guides at the expense of conversion clarity.
3. **Rule 3: Editorial Cross-Linking Funnels Intent:**
   Every Informational Guide must include at least two prominent, contextual calls to action pointing directly to its corresponding Transactional Pillar (e.g. Wedding Rituals Guide &rarr; Wedding Pandit Booking Page).
