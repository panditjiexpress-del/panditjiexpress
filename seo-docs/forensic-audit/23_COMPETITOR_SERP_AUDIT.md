# Competitor & Local SERP Comparison — Pandit Ji Express

**Audit Date:** September 27, 2026  
**Target Query:** "North Indian Pandit in Bangalore", "Hindi Pandit Bangalore"  

---

## 1. Primary Competitor Landscape

1. **Aggregator Directories (JustDial, Sulekha, Urban Company):**
   - *Strengths:* Massive domain authority, millions of backlinks.
   - *Weaknesses:* Thin generic listings, lack of religious authenticity, middlemen fees, no verified individual purohit bios.
2. **Local Pandit Portals (SmartPuja, PujaNPujari):**
   - *Strengths:* Broad catalog of pujas across South and North Indian traditions.
   - *Weaknesses:* Corporate agency feel; customer rarely knows which priest will arrive.
3. **Pandit Ji Express Strategic Advantage:**
   - **Authentic Purohit Leadership:** Direct booking with Pandit Shyam Sundar.
   - **North Indian Specialization:** Tailored exclusively to Hindi, Bihari, Maithil, and UP cultural traditions.
   - **Complete Transparency:** Upfront samagri checklists, pricing transparency, and zero hidden costs.
