## 1. Distinguishing the Four Core SEO Problems

| Problem Type | Definition | Pandit Ji Express Status | Remediation Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Indexation Problem** | Page cannot be crawled or is excluded by Google | **Affects newest 6 pages** (due to crawl lag & link starvation) | Add contextual inlinks; submit to GSC |
| **B. Ranking Problem** | Page is indexed but appears on page 3–5 | **Affects competitive terms** ("pandit in bangalore") | Local citations, Google Business Profile reviews |
| **C. Query Mismatch Problem**| Page ranks for unintended query | **Minor risk** on homepage vs best-pandit guide | Refine page titles and H1 modifiers |
| **D. CTR Problem** | High impressions, low clicks | **Healthy CTR** on long-tail branded queries | Optimize meta descriptions with clear phone and WhatsApp CTAs |
