## 2. How to Prevent Cannibalization Drift
1. **Never alter titles to cross-target broad queries:** Keep `/best-north-indian-pandit-bangalore` strictly focused on credentials, vetting, and comparison.
2. **Anchor Text Discipline:** Internal links targeting `/` must use brand and general terms ("Pandit Ji Express", "North Indian Pandit in Bangalore"). Internal links targeting `/best-north-indian-pandit-bangalore` must use modifier anchors ("Best Vedic Pandits", "top rated pandits in Bangalore").
