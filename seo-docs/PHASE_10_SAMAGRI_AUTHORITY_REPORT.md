Documented the scriptural and physiological reasons behind sacred dravyas:

- *Cinnamomum camphora (Bhimseni Kapoor)*: Natural unrefined crystalline camphor that sublimates 100% cleanly without petroleum residue or toxic black soot.
- *Commiphora mukul (Guggul) & Boswellia (Loban)*: Sacred resins from Atharvaveda and Charaka Samhita that calm the nervous system and elevate indoor prana.
- *Go-Ghrita (Pure Cow Ghee)*: Supreme *Havyavahana* carrying mantras to devatas, strictly avoiding hydrogenated vanaspati.
- *Akshat (Unbroken Rice)*: Strict rejection of broken rice to symbolize non-decaying cosmic completeness.
- *Desmostachya bipinnata (Kusha/Darbha)*: Natural energetic insulator during Sankalpa.

### C. Apartment-Friendly Low-Smoke Protocol

3. **Cross-Ventilation Safety:** Respecting apartment building fire safety rules without triggering building alarms.
