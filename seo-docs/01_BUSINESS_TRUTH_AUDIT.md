### 3.2 Prohibited Inventions & Claims Audit
The codebase was scanned for prohibited marketing claims:
* **Degrees / Certifications**: Zero false claims of "PhD in Sanskrit", "Certified Vedic Priest by Govt of India", or fictitious astrological awards.
* **Priest Profiles**: Removed legacy references to fictional priests (e.g. `pandit-rahul-shastri.html` was safely 301-redirected to `pandit-shyam-sundar`).
* **Guarantees**: Scanned and purged of all absolute guarantees ("100% Guaranteed Success", "Guaranteed Prosperity", "Guaranteed Boy Child", "Guaranteed Wealth").
* **Physical Offices**: The business does NOT claim storefronts in 20 Bangalore localities. It is correctly represented as a **Service-Area Business (SAB)** based out of Jakkur/Srirampura covering Greater Bangalore.
* **Customer Counts**: No inflated claims such as "100,000+ happy families" or fake celebrity endorsements.
