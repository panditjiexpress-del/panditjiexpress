## 3. Content Publication Governance Rules

To preserve search quality and avoid content dilution:
1. **No Rushed Spindles:** Every new page must meet the 2,000+ word depth threshold with verified cultural and local details.
2. **Distinct Search Intent:** No new page will be launched unless it targets a validated, non-cannibalizing keyword cluster.
3. **Owner Verification Pre-requisite:** Service availability and operational logistics must be validated before writing.
