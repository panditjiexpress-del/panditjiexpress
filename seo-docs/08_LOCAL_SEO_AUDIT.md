`https://panditjiexpress.in/areas-we-serve`

### 3.2 Doorway Page Prevention Rules
To maintain strict compliance with Google's Quality Guidelines and avoid "doorway page" penalties:
1. **No Programmatic Text Replacement:** Never clone a page and merely replace "Whitefield" with "Indiranagar".
2. **Mandatory Unique Local Elements:** Every locality page must include:
   * Neighborhood-specific context (e.g. IT corridor tech schedules, gated community apartment havan permissions).
   * Realistic travel logistics and arrival timing from North Bangalore base.
   * Specific residential societies frequently served (e.g. Prestige Shantiniketan in Whitefield, Sobha Classic in HSR).
   * Verified local client inquiries and customary ritual scheduling.
3. **Threshold for Creating New Locality Pages:** New locality pages (e.g. Electronic City, Sarjapur Road, Hebbal) will only be created when authentic local demand and differentiated local information are available.
