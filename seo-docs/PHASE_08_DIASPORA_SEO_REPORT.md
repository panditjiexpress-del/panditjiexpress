## 3. Cultural & Shastric Authenticity Enhancements

### 3.1 Bihari Pandit Authority (`/bihari-pandit-bangalore`)
* **Chhath Puja Mahaparv:** Comprehensive Shastric structure covering all 4 days (Nahay-Khay bottle gourd purification, Kharna Rasiya kheer, Sandhya Arghya sunset offerings with daura, and Usha Arghya sunrise offerings with thekua prasad and sugarcane).
* **Bihari Vivah Traditions:** Detailed breakdown including Tilak, Mandapachhadana, Kanyadaan, Laja Homa (parched rice ahuti with bride's brother), Saat Phere, and traditional long Sindoor Daan (nose-to-forehead) per Bhojpuri and Awadhi traditions.
* **Satyanarayan Vrat Katha:** Authentic Kasar (roasted wheat flour panjiri), panchamrit, banana trunk mandap, and Reva Khanda recitation.
* **Gaya Shraadh & Pitru Paksha:** Tarpan and Pind Daan with kusha grass, barley, and black sesame (til) following the Shukla Yajurvedic Madhyandina Shakha.

### 3.2 Maithil Pandit Authority (`/maithil-pandit-bangalore`)
* **Mithila Vivah Sanskar:** Grounded in Siddhant verification (Gotra and Mool ancestry check), Kanyadaan, Laja Homa, Saat Phere, and the sacred Kohbar Ghar ceremonies with Madhuparka and Maithili devotional singing.
* **Janeu Sanskar (Upanayanam):** Officiated strictly according to the Mithila Paddhati of Shukla Yajurveda, including Chudakaran, Yagnopavita dharana (3 to 6 strands), Brahmacharya vows, Bhikshacharanam, and secret Gayatri Diksha.
* **Madhushravani Puja:** Detailed 13-day monsoon vrata for newlywed brides, featuring daily Manasa and Shiva-Parvati katha recitations and concluding Temi daghana rituals.
* **Sanitized Delivery Language:** Removed all legacy "doorstep samagri delivery" claims; normalized to complete ceremony samagri kits brought by Pandit Ji upon arrival.

### 3.3 Hindi-Speaking North Indian Pandit Authority (`/hindi-speaking-pandit-bangalore`)
* **Bilingual Sanskrit-Hindi Synthesis:** Articulated the theological necessity of chanting mantras in pure Vedic Sanskrit (vibrational Shabda Brahma) while providing clear Hindi explanations so children, youth, and elders understand the spiritual meaning.
