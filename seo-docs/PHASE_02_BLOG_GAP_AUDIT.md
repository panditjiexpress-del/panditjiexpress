## 3. Implementation Plan & Production Standards

For each of the 10 BUILD guides, the article must deliver:
1. **Target Word Count:** 2,000–3,500 words of authentic Vedic, cultural, and local substance.
2. **AEO Direct Answer Box (`.blog-direct-answer`):** Clear, 40–60 word extractable snippet for AI search engines.
3. **Structured HTML Elements:** Step-by-step ordered lists, semantic comparison tables, and highlighted safety callouts.
4. **Interactive FAQ Accordion:** Minimum 5–6 relevant questions with matching `FAQPage` schema.
5. **Bidirectional Internal Linking:** Linking up to its parent pillar, laterally to cluster siblings, down to locality hubs, and forward to `/booking`.
6. **Strict Schema Markup:** JSON-LD `@graph` with `BlogPosting` + `BreadcrumbList` + `FAQPage` + `Author` (Pandit Shyam Sundar).
