## 3. Anti-Cannibalization Protocol & Keyword Separation

To prevent internal keyword competition between commercial service pages and educational guides, the following semantic boundaries are enforced:

1. **Wedding Cluster:**
   * **Commercial Target (`/wedding-pandit-bangalore`):** Captures `wedding pandit in bangalore`, `vivah sanskar purohit`, `hire wedding pandit`. Focuses on booking dates, venue locations, and package scope.
   * **Educational Target (`/north-indian-wedding-rituals-bangalore`):** Captures `north indian wedding rituals`, `saptapadi meaning`, `7 steps of hindu marriage`. Focuses on cultural meaning, vidhi order, and family preparation.
2. **Griha Pravesh Cluster:**
   * **Commercial Target (`/griha-pravesh-pooja-bangalore`):** Captures `griha pravesh pandit in bangalore`, `housewarming puja booking`. Focuses on pricing, priest arrival, and havan setup.
   * **Educational Target (`/griha-pravesh-puja-bangalore-guide`):** Captures `griha pravesh puja process`, `milk boiling shubh muhurat rules`. Focuses on step-by-step rituals and apartment rules.
3. **Samagri Cluster:**
   * **Commercial Target (`/samagri`):** Captures `pooja samagri in bangalore`, `puja kits arranged`. Focuses on kit arrangements and service inclusions.
   * **Educational Target (`/puja-samagri-list-bangalore`):** Captures `puja samagri list`, `hindu ceremony checklist`. Focuses on exhaustive item counts and botanical Sanskrit terms.
4. **General & Selection Cluster:**
   * **Brand/Local Root (`/`):** Primary local search landing page for general search `north indian pandit in bangalore`.
   * **Buyer Guide (`/best-north-indian-pandit-bangalore`):** Informational decision guide for `how to choose a pandit`, `finding trusted pandit ji`.
   * **Cost Transparency (`/pandit-cost-bangalore`):** Dedicated commercial investigation guide for `pandit cost`, `puja dakshina charges in bangalore`.
