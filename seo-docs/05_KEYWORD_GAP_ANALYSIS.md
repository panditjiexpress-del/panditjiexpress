## 2. Priority Keyword Gaps to Address in Next Phases

1. **Electronic City Locality Hub:** High demand for apartment Griha Pravesh and Satyanarayan Pujas.
2. **Sarjapur Road Locality Hub:** Large influx of North Indian home buyers moving into new gated communities.
3. **North Bangalore / Hebbal-Jakkur Local Authority:** Leveraging physical proximity to Jakkur base for rapid response.
4. **Vastu Shanti Specific Query Clustering:** Differentiating standalone Vastu Shanti from new home Griha Pravesh.
5. **Festival Spikes (Chhath Puja, Diwali Lakshmi Puja, Karwa Chauth):** High seasonal query volume requiring timed supporting guides.
