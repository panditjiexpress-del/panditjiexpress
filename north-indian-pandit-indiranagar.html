Planning a puja in Indiranagar

When enquiring, include your street or nearby landmark, whether the ceremony is at a home, apartment, hall or workplace, the preferred date and approximate start time. Families can also mention their language preference, family tradition or gotra, the ceremony they have in mind, and whether they want the priest to arrange puja samagri. The priest can then discuss the appropriate vidhi and what should be prepared before the muhurat.

Indiranagar is served by the Purple Line of Namma Metro and has well-known corridors such as 100 Feet Road and CMH Road. Use the exact venue address in your enquiry rather than assuming all Indiranagar locations have the same access or travel time. Booking confirmation should account for the venue and date.

Apartment, home and workplace arrangements

For an apartment puja or havan, check the building’s fire-safety and community rules before confirming an open-flame ritual. Share any restrictions with the booking team so the ceremony setup can be discussed in advance. For independent homes, halls or businesses, tell us about the available ceremony space and venue requirements. Never assume a havan is permitted in a balcony or common area without the property’s approval.
