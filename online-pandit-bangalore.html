Shastric Boundaries: When to Choose In-Person vs. Virtual E-Puja

In Sanatana Dharma, the spiritual merit of a ritual depends on the sincerity of the Yajamana (performer) and the correct vibrational recitation of the Purohit. While digital communication bridges geographical distances, Pandit Ji Express adheres to strict Shastric boundaries regarding what can and cannot be conducted virtually:

  • Suitable for Remote E-Puja: Shri Satyanarayan Vrat Katha, Navagraha Japa, Mahamrityunjaya Anushthan, Birthday/Anniversary blessings, and planetary dosha nivaran pujas where the family performs offerings at their home altar guided by Pandit Ji on live video.
  • Mandatory In-Person Presence: Major milestone life-cycle sacraments (Vivah Sanskar, Griha Pravesh, and Upanayanam Janeu) strictly require physical in-person presence for physical threshold crossing, Kanyadaan hand-joining, and physical Agni circumambulation (Saat Phere).
